Last updated: 19 September 2026 · Italiano · Français

The data controller is Maria Laura Malinconi, Via Luigi Ornato 82, Milan, Italy. Contact for any request about your data: marna.business12@gmail.com.

3. Minimum age

MARNA is intended for users aged 16 or over, which you confirm when signing up. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 16: if we learn that an account belongs to a person under 16, we deactivate it and delete the associated data.

4. Data visible to other users

Username, display name, bio, level, streak, public playlists and the song you are listening to are visible to other App users. You can block and report other users from their profile. Reports are reviewed within 24 hours.

5. Who receives your data

We do not sell your data. We share it only with providers needed to run the App, acting as processors or independent controllers:

  • Supabase — database, authentication and server functions. The MARNA project is hosted in the European Union (Ireland).
  • Apple — Sign in with Apple, Apple Music catalogue and previews, music recognition (ShazamKit), App Store.
  • Google — Sign in with Google, Google AdMob and Google User Messaging Platform (ads and consent), Google Play.
  • RevenueCat — subscription and in-app purchase management.

6. Transfers outside the EU

Google, Apple and RevenueCat may process data outside the European Economic Area, in particular in the United States. Transfers rely on a European Commission adequacy decision (EU-US Data Privacy Framework) or on Standard Contractual Clauses.

7. How long we keep data

  • Account and App state data: as long as your account is active. When you delete your account they are deleted without delay; technical backup copies may remain for a limited period at the provider before being overwritten.
  • Reports: for as long as needed to handle them and protect the community, and in any case no more than 24 months after closure.
  • Purchase data: kept by Apple, Google and RevenueCat under their legal and tax obligations.

8. Your rights

You can at any time ask for access to your data, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing and data portability, and you can object to processing based on legitimate interest. You can withdraw consent at any time without affecting earlier processing. Write to marna.business12@gmail.com: we reply within one month.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Italian data protection authority (Garante per la protezione dei dati personali, www.garanteprivacy.it) or with the supervisory authority of your country of residence.

9. Users outside Italy and the European Union

MARNA is available in many countries. We handle data with the same safeguards wherever you are and, where local law gives you additional rights, we honour them:

  • United Kingdom: you have the rights under the UK GDPR and can contact the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).
  • United States (e.g. California and other states with privacy laws): you can ask to know what data we hold about you, to correct or delete it, and to opt out of the sale or "sharing" of data for targeted advertising. We do not sell your data for money; ads shown through Google AdMob may count as targeted advertising and you can decline them in the ad consent screen or your device settings. We do not discriminate against anyone exercising these rights.
  • Brazil, Canada, Australia, Japan, South Korea and other countries: you can exercise the rights granted by your local data protection law by writing to the contact in section 1.

We do not use microphone data or other sensitive data for advertising, and we do not collect data from people under 16. If the law of your country requires a higher minimum age or parental consent to use online services, you must not use the App.

10. Deleting your account

You can delete your account and associated data in the App (Profile → Settings → Account → Delete account) or by following the instructions on How to delete your account.

11. Security

Communication with our servers uses encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS), and access to data is limited by row-level security rules in the database so each user can only modify their own data.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change how we process data we will update this page and the date above, and notify you in the App for significant changes.
