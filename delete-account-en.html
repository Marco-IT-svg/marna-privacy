How to delete your MARNA account

Last updated: 19 September 2026 · Italiano · Français

From the app (fastest)

  1. Open MARNA and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile → Settings → Account → Delete account.
  3. Confirm. Your account is deleted immediately and, if you signed in with Apple, the Apple sign-in access is revoked.

If you cannot access the app

Email marna.business12@gmail.com from the address you registered with, with the subject "MARNA account deletion". We will reply and delete your account within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your profile (email, username, name, bio), level, XP and streak.
  • Playlists, saved songs, music diary, preferences and other synchronised settings.
  • Friendships, messages and blocked users.

What may be kept

  • Reports concerning community safety, for as long as needed to handle them (24 months at most).
  • Purchase records kept by Apple, Google and RevenueCat for tax and legal obligations. Deleting your account does not cancel subscriptions: cancel them in your App Store or Google Play account before deleting your account.
  • Technical backup copies, which our provider overwrites within a short time.

For more information see the Privacy Policy.
